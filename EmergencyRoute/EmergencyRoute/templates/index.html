Dijkstra's Algorithm

Guarantees mathematical optimality by greedily relaxing vertices extracted from a Min-Heap. With $W(u, v) = \frac{d}{v_{eff}} \times 60$, it steers ambulances away from gridlock toward high-speed emergency expressways.

A* Search with Admissible Heuristic

Uses $f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$ where $h(n) = \frac{\text{haversine}(n, \text{target})}{v_{max}} \times 60$. Because straight-line distance at top speed never overestimates real travel time ($h(n) \le h^*(n)$), A* finds the optimal path while exploring fewer nodes.

Why Greedy Search Fails

Greedy Best-First orders vertices solely on proximity to the hospital $h(n)$. It can rush toward an arterial road only to get trapped by a complete roadblock or heavy traffic bottleneck, causing longer delays.
